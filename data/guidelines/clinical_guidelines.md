# Clinical Utilization Guidelines (Synthetic, Coursework Benchmark)

GL-MRI-3.1 Lumbar spine MRI is indicated for low back pain accompanied by documented red-flag features (severe or progressive neurological deficit, suspected cauda equina syndrome, suspected spinal infection, or malignancy), or when persistent pain lasts 6 weeks or more despite documented adherence to conservative therapy (physiotherapy, medication).
GL-MRI-3.2 Routine MRI of the lumbar spine is not indicated for acute uncomplicated low back pain lasting under 6 weeks without documented red-flag features.
GL-KNEE-2.1 Knee arthroscopy is indicated for meniscal tears with persistent mechanical symptoms (joint locking, catching, or giving way) after at least 6 weeks of failed conservative care; it is not indicated for degenerative osteoarthritis alone in the absence of mechanical obstruction.
GL-KNEE-2.2 Knee MRI is indicated for persistent knee pain lasting 6 weeks or more despite documented conservative management, or immediately following acute severe knee trauma with inability to bear weight.
GL-TKR-4.1 Total knee replacement (arthroplasty) is indicated for advanced severe osteoarthritis with radiographic joint space narrowing, persistent functional impairment, and failure of at least 3 months of comprehensive conservative management.
GL-BAR-5.1 Bariatric surgery is indicated for adult patients with a Body Mass Index (BMI) of 40 or greater, or BMI of 35 or greater accompanied by at least one severe obesity-related comorbidity (e.g., uncontrolled type 2 diabetes, severe obstructive sleep apnea), plus at least 6 months of documented supervised weight-loss attempts.
GL-ONC-6.1 Chemotherapy and antineoplastic regimens require a histopathologically confirmed malignancy diagnosis and a detailed written treatment regimen signed by a board-certified medical oncologist.
GL-PT-7.1 Physical therapy is medically indicated following orthopedic surgery, acute musculoskeletal trauma, or documented functional deficit causing significant limitation in activities of daily living.
GL-CT-8.1 Head CT scanning is indicated for acute head trauma accompanied by red-flag criteria: documented loss of consciousness, repeated episodes of vomiting, retrograde amnesia exceeding 30 minutes, or ongoing antiplatelet/anticoagulant therapy.
GL-ENT-9.1 Nasal surgery (rhinoplasty/septoplasty) is medically necessary only for documented structural airway obstruction confirmed by clinical rhinoscopy or significant post-traumatic nasal deformity causing respiratory impairment; procedures performed purely for aesthetic or cosmetic contouring are not medically necessary.
