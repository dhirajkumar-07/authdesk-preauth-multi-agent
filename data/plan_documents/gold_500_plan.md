# SynthCare Health Plan: Gold-500 Comprehensive Benefit Policy (Synthetic)

## Section 1: Plan Deductibles and Cost Sharing
PLAN-1.1 The Gold-500 Comprehensive Health Benefit Plan covers medically necessary services with an annual deductible of $500 per individual member and $1,000 per family.

## Section 2: Covered Outpatient & Inpatient Services
PLAN-2.1 Diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans) is covered when medically necessary and ordered by an in-network provider. Prior authorisation is mandatory.
PLAN-2.2 Inpatient and outpatient orthopedic and general surgeries, including arthroscopy and joint replacement, are covered when clinical necessity criteria are met. Prior authorisation is required.
PLAN-2.3 Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and rehabilitation are covered up to 30 sessions per plan year upon written referral from a licensed physician.
PLAN-2.4 Bariatric surgical procedures (gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy) are covered only when clinical guideline thresholds are verified. Prior authorisation is mandatory.
PLAN-2.5 Reconstructive nasal surgery (septoplasty/rhinoplasty) is covered only when performed to correct severe airway obstruction or severe post-traumatic nasal deformity. Prior authorisation is required.

## Section 3: Oncology and Specialty Therapeutics
PLAN-3.1 Chemotherapy, radiation oncology, and targeted cancer treatments are covered with prior authorisation when prescribed under an oncologist's formal written treatment plan.
PLAN-3.2 Advanced biologic medications, specialty pharmaceuticals, and intravenous infusion therapies require prior authorisation and clinical review.

## Section 4: General Exclusions
PLAN-4.1 Cosmetic procedures, surgeries, and treatments performed solely to improve appearance without functional medical necessity are strictly excluded from coverage.
PLAN-4.2 Experimental, investigational, or unproven treatments (including non-FDA approved stem-cell therapies and regenerative injections) are strictly excluded from coverage.
PLAN-4.3 Services rendered by out-of-network healthcare providers or facilities are excluded from coverage, except in verified life-threatening medical emergencies.
PLAN-4.4 Dental procedures, cosmetic dentistry, and routine oral surgeries are excluded from medical coverage, except for immediate repair of acute traumatic facial injuries.

## Section 7: Prior Authorisation & Utilization Management
PLAN-7.1 Prior authorisation is required before the delivery of elective surgeries, advanced imaging, bariatric procedures, oncology regimens, and specialty prescription drugs.
PLAN-7.2 Incomplete Clinical Submissions: If submitted medical records lack documentation required to determine medical necessity, the plan must issue a request for additional clinical information (MORE_INFO) before a determination can be made.
PLAN-7.3 Clinical Peer Review and Appeals: Any adverse determination (denial) is subject to mandatory clinical peer review escalation by a licensed physician before finalization, and members retain full appeal rights within 60 calendar days.
