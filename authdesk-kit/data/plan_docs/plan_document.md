# SynthCare Health Plan: Member Benefits Document (synthetic, for coursework)

## Section 2: Covered Services
PLAN-2.1 Diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT, PET) is covered when medically necessary and ordered by an in-network provider. Prior authorisation is required.
PLAN-2.2 Inpatient and outpatient surgery, including arthroscopy and joint replacement, is covered when medically necessary. Prior authorisation is required.
PLAN-2.3 Physiotherapy is covered up to 20 sessions per plan year with a physician referral.
PLAN-2.4 Bariatric surgery is covered only when the clinical guideline criteria are met. Prior authorisation is required.

## Section 3: Oncology
PLAN-3.1 Chemotherapy and related oncology treatment are covered with prior authorisation when prescribed under an oncologist's treatment plan.

## Section 4: Exclusions
PLAN-4.1 Cosmetic procedures performed to improve appearance without a functional medical need are excluded.
PLAN-4.2 Experimental, investigational or unproven treatments are excluded.
PLAN-4.3 Services from out-of-network providers are excluded except in emergencies.

## Section 7: Prior Authorisation Process
PLAN-7.1 Prior authorisation is required for imaging, surgery, bariatric procedures, and specialty drugs.
PLAN-7.2 If a request lacks the clinical documentation needed to assess it, the plan must request additional information before deciding.
