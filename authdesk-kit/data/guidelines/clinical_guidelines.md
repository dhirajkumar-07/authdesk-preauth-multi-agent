# Clinical Guidelines (synthetic, for coursework only, not real medical advice)

GL-MRI-3.1 Lumbar spine MRI is indicated for low back pain with red-flag features (e.g. neurological deficit, suspected infection or cancer), or when pain persists 6 weeks or more despite documented conservative therapy.
GL-MRI-3.2 Routine MRI is not indicated for acute uncomplicated low back pain lasting under 6 weeks without red-flag features.
GL-KNEE-2.1 Knee arthroscopy is indicated for meniscal tears with mechanical symptoms after failed conservative care. It is not indicated for degenerative osteoarthritis alone.
GL-TKR-4.1 Total knee replacement is indicated for severe osteoarthritis with functional impairment after at least 3 months of failed conservative treatment.
GL-BAR-5.1 Bariatric surgery is indicated for BMI 40 or above, or BMI 35 or above with a serious comorbidity, plus documented supervised weight-loss attempts.
GL-ONC-6.1 Chemotherapy requires a histologically confirmed cancer diagnosis and a written oncologist treatment plan.
GL-PT-7.1 Physiotherapy is indicated after surgery or for musculoskeletal injury with a documented functional deficit.
GL-CT-8.1 CT head is indicated for head trauma with red-flag features (loss of consciousness, vomiting, new neurological deficit, anticoagulant use).
